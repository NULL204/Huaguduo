# 花骨朵 — character bible

Original virtual singer (V家-style). **NOT Luo Tianyi, NOT Hatsune Miku, not any existing character** — no grey
hair, no jade/green hair ornaments, no teal twin tails. Internal name: **阿朵 A-Duo** ("the bud").

| part | spec |
|---|---|
| hair | waist-length straight hair, ink black (#1B1420) with a carmine inner colour (#C8183C → #E85A74) on the underside and tips; blunt bangs with a few see-through strands; one thin braid on her LEFT side |
| eyes | garnet red (#8E1B2E) fading to amber (#F2A35C) at the bottom of the iris; calm, slightly downcast default |
| signature | a closed **poppy-bud hairpin** (carmine bud in two green sepals) on her LEFT side above the braid; a small carmine **花钿** (three-petal mark) on her forehead |
| outfit | white sleeveless top with a high mandarin collar and one carmine knot button (盘扣); white sheer long sleeves (separate arm covers) dyed carmine from the fingertips up to the elbow; charcoal-black pleated knee skirt with a thin carmine hem line; black tights; black Mary Jane shoes |
| motion prop | a long translucent carmine **披帛** (silk shawl) draped over both elbows and across her back, fading to white at both ends, streaming in the wind |
| palette | ink #1B1420 · carmine #C8183C · rouge pink #E85A74 · paper white #F4EEE4 · skin #FFF1EA · sepal green #6E9A64 (hairpin only) · charcoal #2A2630 |
| key colour | **blue #0000FF** for transparent jobs (the design has green sepals and pinks, so neither green nor magenta keys are safe; nothing in the design is pure blue) |
